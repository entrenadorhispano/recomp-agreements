Account and Data Deletion Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

At Recomp, we respect your privacy and give you control over your personal data. This page outlines how you can request the deletion of your account and associated data stored via our Firebase backend.

1. How to Delete Your Account from Within the App

If you have the Recomp app installed on your device, you can delete your account directly:

  1. Open the Recomp app and log in to your account.
  2. Go to your Profile or Settings menu.
  3. Select Delete Account and confirm your decision.

Once confirmed, your user profile, authentication credentials, and associated fitness records stored in Firebase will be permanently deleted from our active databases.

2. How to Request Deletion via Email (Alternative Method)

If you cannot access the app or prefer to request deletion manually, you can contact us directly:

Email us at: support@recomp.app (or your contact email)

Subject: Account Deletion Request - Recomp

Body: Please include the registered email address associated with your Recomp account. We will process your request and confirm data deletion within 7 business days.

3. Data Retained and Erased

  • Data Deleted: Firebase Authentication records, user profile details, and personal training logs/macros stored in Cloud Firestore.
  • Data Retention: We do not retain any personal data after your account deletion request is fully processed, except where required by law.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding data privacy or account deletion, feel free to reach out through our support channels.
